Hi!

I’m Ashwin.

I call this blog The Halli Brief because my last name ends in “Halli” (which means village in my native language) and since the full version is quite long, I call it The Halli Brief.

It’s a place for me to share what I’m learning and doing, mostly in short, direct posts that get straight to the point.

I live in the Bay Area and lead the Identity and Access Management (IAM) team for a company. My technical writing usually covers what I currently am learning.

My interests outside of work are pretty eclectic. I spend my time biking, snowboarding, and video gaming. I also enjoy cooking and have spent a lot of time perfecting a banana bread recipe that uses a mix of almond and regular flour to get the texture just right.

Best way to get in touch with me is via linkedIn.

Thanks for reading!
